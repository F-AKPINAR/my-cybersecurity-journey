# Week 1 — Introduction to Cybersecurity

## 📖 Topics I Learned

### Introduction to Cybersecurity
- Cybersecurity: the discipline of protecting information, systems, and networks against unauthorized access, attacks, and damage.
- **The CIA Triad:** Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability — the three core principles of security.
- Types of attackers and their motivations; the concepts of defense (blue team) and offense (red team).

### Cyber Kill Chain
The step-by-step stages of an attack:
1. **Reconnaissance** — gathering information about the target
2. **Weaponization** — preparing the malicious payload
3. **Delivery** — getting the payload to the target
4. **Exploitation** — abusing a vulnerability
5. **Installation** — establishing persistent access
6. **Command & Control (C2)** — remote management
7. **Actions on Objectives** — achieving the real goal

> Defensive idea: if you **break any single link** in the chain, you stop the attack.

### Incident Response Life Cycle
A structured response to a security incident:
- **Preparation** → **Detection & Analysis** → **Containment** → **Eradication** → **Recovery** → **Lessons Learned**

## 💡 Key Takeaways
- Attacks aren't random; they follow a **staged process** — knowing this makes planning a defense easier.
- Security isn't just "not getting hacked"; it means protecting confidentiality, integrity, and availability all together.
